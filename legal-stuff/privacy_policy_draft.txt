================================================================================
DATA HANDLING AND PRIVACY POLICY (INTERNAL WORKING DRAFT)
================================================================================

This document outlines how telemetry data, user session metrics, and diagnostic 
logs are collected, stored, and processed within the TelemetryCore workspace.

DATA COLLECTION PRACTICES:
- We collect standard connection metadata, including client user-agent strings, 
  timestamp logs, and packet throughput rates to optimize dashboard performance.
- Diagnostic data gathered during debugging sessions is restricted to local 
  development environments and is not shared with third-party tracking services.

DATA RETENTION:
Log files generated by background telemetry loops are automatically purged after 
a rolling 30-day window unless flagged for explicit anomaly analysis.

USER RIGHTS:
Contributors and reviewers retain the right to audit local commit histories and 
request clarification on data parsing scripts via repository issue trackers.
================================================================================
